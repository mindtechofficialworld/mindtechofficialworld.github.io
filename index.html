    Guides

    TalkBack basics

    • Touch the screen to hear what is under your finger.
    • Swipe right to move to the next item and swipe left to go back.
    • Double tap anywhere to activate the item you last heard.
    • Scroll with two fingers, swiping up or down.
    • On many Android phones, holding both volume keys for three seconds turns TalkBack on or off.

    Helpful apps

    • Be My Eyes: connects blind and low-vision people with sighted volunteers by video call.
    • Google Lookout: uses the camera to read text and describe things around you on Android.
    • Seeing AI: Microsoft app that reads text and describes scenes on iPhone.
    • NVDA: a free screen reader for Windows computers.

    Tips for accessible content

    • Use real headings, so screen reader users can jump between sections.
    • Give every button and form field a clear text label.
    • Never rely on color alone to give information.

      About AccessHub

      AccessHub is an independent, volunteer-style project that wants every person to use technology with confidence, whether they use a screen reader, magnification or no assistive technology at all.

      Our principles

      • Screen reader first: every feature works with TalkBack.
      • Simple forms, big buttons, clear spoken results.
      • Free to use. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Settings and notes stay on your phone.
